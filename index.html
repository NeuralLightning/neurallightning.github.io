The Problem

Would you know what to do?

Governments produce civil defence guidance. The problem is that most people cannot access it at the moment they need it. During a sudden emergency, who has time to find the right website, navigate to the correct page, and read through it carefully enough to act?

The gap is not one of provision but of access. Civil Defence Assistant is the last mile, from official source to citizen in a crisis.

Civil Defence Assistant

Plain-language guidance from official sources only

Civil Defence Assistant delivers immediate responses to civil emergency queries, drawn exclusively from curated, official civil defence documentation pre-loaded on the device. It does not search the open internet. It does not generate generic advice.

Official sources

Answers draw exclusively from curated official documentation — government agencies, emergency services, statutory bodies. Nothing else.

Grounded answers

Every response is verified against the official corpus. Answers that cannot be grounded in it are suppressed rather than generated from general knowledge.

Works offline

Designed to operate in communications-denied and bandwidth-constrained environments. Civil Defence Assistant adapts automatically to available connectivity.

Multilingual

Corpus and user language are independent. Any corpus can be queried in any supported language.

Use case one

During an active incident

A citizen faces a flooding event, a power cut, or a security situation. They need to know what to do, now. Civil Defence Assistant responds to plain-language questions in seconds, with or without connectivity.

Use case two

Before an incident

A citizen wants to prepare their household for the kinds of emergencies relevant to their area. Civil Defence Assistant draws on official guidance to answer their questions at their own pace.
