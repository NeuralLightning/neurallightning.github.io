Privacy

This page covers the only data this site collects: what you submit through the contact form.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, organisation, email address, and message you provide. We do not use cookies, analytics, or any other tracking on this site.

How it's used

Submissions are processed by our form provider, Formspree (their privacy policy), and delivered to a shared Neural Lightning inbox. We use it only to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell or share it with third parties beyond Formspree's role in delivering the message to us.

Questions

If you have questions about this, or want your submitted data deleted, contact us at contact@neurallightning.com.
